## Evidence-based handoff

Use plain, professional language. Lead with the actual study and requested action, then give the facts that let an independent reviewer understand its objective, target grounding, authority, and constraints. Explain why the selected method and controls fit the scientific question, including for adversarial work. Preserve meaningful technical terms instead of hiding the study's nature. Do not open with unsupported exemptions, pile up generic safety assurances, demand a classification result, or quote provider rules without applying them to the actual task.

```text
Research objective: [exact question and deliverable]
Requested action: [specific bounded assistance or experiment]
Study model: [documented facts, or unknown]
Assistant and evaluator roles: [documented facts]
Study target and environment: [approved target, authorized data, fixtures, tools, and destinations]
Activities and risk characteristics: [actual methods, including adversarial behavior under study]
Authorization: [target authority, current rules or exceptions, and their locators]
Controls and rationale: [containment, retained safeguards, and why they fit the experiment]
Verification: [raw artifact, independent metric, attack and benign controls, and completion test]
Unresolved issues: [unknown facts, restrictions, or skipped dimensions]
```
