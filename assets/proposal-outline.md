## One-sentence thesis

State the problem, proposed contribution, method, and expected value without unsupported certainty.

## Summary

- One-page logic summary (need, gap, goal, objectives, methods, evidence plan, impact)
- Need and gap
- Objectives
- Approach
- Expected intellectual contribution
- Expected practical or broader impact

## Background and gap

Establish the problem, synthesize nearest work, and state the bounded gap with evidence IDs.

## Research questions and objectives

Map each objective to a question, deliverable, success measure, and decision it enables.

## Approach and work packages

For each work package record inputs, methods, outputs, owner, dependencies, milestones, evaluation, and fallback.

## Evaluation and success criteria

Map claims to constructs, datasets or participants, baselines, metrics, uncertainty, ablations or controls, and failure criteria.

## Schedule, resources, and management

Include milestones, critical path, compute and data access, roles, governance, communication, and the budget case (personnel effort, compute, storage, API, data, indirect costs, and its mapping to work packages).

## Risks and alternatives

Include scientific, technical, operational, ethical, adoption, and schedule risks with triggers and mitigations.

## Ethics, data, artifacts, and dissemination

Address approvals, consent, privacy, licenses, security, reproducibility, release constraints, and impact pathways.

## Expected outcomes and limitations

Separate expected artifacts from unearned predicted results. State what success would and would not establish.

## References

Include only verified sources and conform to the required format. Mark unresolved gaps with `[CITATION NEEDED]`, `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]`, or `[RESULT PENDING]` instead of plausible filler.
