## Critical and major findings

For each finding state severity (`critical` or `major`), location, the paper's relevant claim or decision, the observed evidence, the validity or interpretation impact, a feasible remedy or decisive test, and your uncertainty or missing information. Separate correctness from preference.

## Minor and editorial findings

For each finding state severity (`minor` or `editorial`), location, evidence, consequence, and an actionable remedy.

## Questions for the authors

Ask only questions whose answers could clarify the paper, change the assessment, or enable a concrete revision. State why each matters.

## Ethics, artifacts, and reproducibility

Assess approvals, data and model provenance, licenses, privacy, risks, disclosure, code/data availability, exact execution path, and mapping from artifacts to claims.
