## Section plan

### Abstract (draft last)

Problem, gap, approach, only verified headline results, contribution, and boundary.

### Introduction

Motivation, precise problem, nearest-work gap, contribution list, and result preview.

### Related work

Organize by comparison dimensions; represent competitors fairly; end with the exact distinction.

### Method / theory / system

Define setting, assumptions, mechanism, algorithm or architecture, complexity, and reproducibility-critical details.

### Experimental design / study method

Explain data, baselines, metrics, protocol, uncertainty, compute, governance, and preregistered deviations.

### Results

Follow research questions; report distributions, effect sizes, uncertainty, null and negative results, robustness, and errors.

### Discussion, limitations, and impacts

Interpret without exceeding evidence; cover alternatives, validity threats, generalization limits, and societal consequences.

### Conclusion

Answer the research question at the supported scope; do not introduce new claims.

## Consistency audit

- Abstract, introduction, results, discussion, and conclusion make the same scoped claims.
- Tables and figures derive from traceable artifacts.
- Every citation and number is verified.
- Limitations and negative evidence are visible.
